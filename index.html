What this is

The sky is the backdrop. The pick is the point.

The sky

A camera in a window over San Francisco shoots the sky around the clock, for days at a time. Every frame is measured, every day is scored, and the best stretches (fog rolling over the ridge, golden hour, storms, the city at night) become short vertical clips.

The pick

Every day, a blend of weather models prices the next day's high or low temperature in a few US cities. Where the model and the market disagree enough, after fees, that disagreement becomes the pick of the day, posted before the market settles and tracked to the end.

How it works

Two pipelines, one frame

The time lapse and the pick are made separately, then meet in a 30-second vertical video. Here's the very short version.

The sky
  1. ShootStills, around the clock, out a window facing the ridge.
  2. MeasureEvery frame indexed: light, colour, motion, where the sun is.
  3. RankEach day scored against clip recipes. The best are kept.
  4. RenderSteadied, smoothed and cut to 9:16 at full resolution.
The pick
  1. ForecastA blend of models gives a probability for every temperature bin.
  2. PriceEach bin's probability is set against the market's ask, fees included.
  3. PickThe clearest edge, YES or NO, becomes the pick of the day.
The video
  1. ComposeThe pick panel and the model-vs-market figure over the day's sky.
  2. PostYouTube and the other platforms, before the market settles.
  3. SettleThe observed high or low decides it. Win or loss, it's logged.
  4. RecordEvery posted pick counts toward the record below.
