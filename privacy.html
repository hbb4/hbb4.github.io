Privacy policy

Last updated November 3, 2026

What the app is

The WX+EV uploader is a private tool run by the owner of the WX+EV YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload the channel owner's own videos, with their titles, descriptions, tags, playlists, thumbnails and captions, to the WX+EV YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google and YouTube data it uses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app asks for the YouTube scopes it needs to upload and manage videos on that channel (youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl). It uses them only to upload videos, set their details and thumbnails, add them to the channel's playlists, and read back the status of videos it uploaded.

The app does not read, collect or store data about any other person, including viewers, commenters or subscribers.

Storage

The sign-in tokens Google issues are stored only on the channel owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server other than Google's, and they are not shared with or sold to anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account › Third-party access; deleting the local token file also removes it from the app.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube's own data handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy, and use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.
